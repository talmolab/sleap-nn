    Distillation¶

    Experimental

    Distillation may change or be removed in a later release without a deprecation period. Its defaults are untuned, and it showed no measured benefit in the developers' tests: check a distilled student against one trained without a teacher on your own data.

    trainer_config.distillation trains a model with help from another, already trained model, the teacher. It follows DWPose (Yang et al. 2023):

    • Stage 1, teacher → student. A big or high-resolution teacher (say ViT-B at 384×288) teaches a smaller or cheaper student (ViT-S at 256×192). The student keeps its usual supervised loss and also learns to match the teacher's features and heatmaps.
    • Stage 2, head self-distillation. The trained student becomes its own teacher: its backbone is frozen, its head is re-initialized and trained to match the old head's heatmaps. DWPose reports a further small gain for little cost.

    Distillation is off by default. It works for single_instance, centered_instance and multi_class_topdown models (the confidence-map head; multi_class_topdown's class vectors are trained as usual), and, as an experimental stage 1, for the box detectors bbox_centernet, bbox_ssd and bbox_detr (see Box detectors).

    Quick start¶

    Train the student with a teacher:

    data_config:
  preprocessing:
    crop_size: [256, 192]            # the student's input
    crop_mode: bbox
    crop_pipeline: warp
trainer_config:
  distillation:
    teacher: /runs/vitpose_b_384     # model directory (best.ckpt + training_config.yaml)
    teacher_crop_size: [384, 288]    # the teacher gets a real 384x288 crop

    (Without the warp crop path, use teacher_input_scale: 1.5 instead; see Teacher resolution.)

    sleap-nn train student.yaml

    Then refine the student's head with its own predictions:

    trainer_config:
  max_steps: 10000                   # stage 2 is short
  distillation:
    stage: 2
    teacher: /runs/vit_s_stage1      # the stage-1 student

    How it works¶

    Stage 1¶

    The loss is

    loss = supervised + r(t) * (alpha * L_feat + beta * L_heatmap)
    • L_heatmap is the MSE between the student's and the teacher's confidence maps over the nodes the two skeletons share by name. A COCO-17 teacher can teach a student with a larger skeleton; nodes the teacher lacks only get the supervised loss. The teacher's maps are resized to the student's output grid.
    • L_feat is the MSE between the teacher's deepest backbone feature and the student's, mapped to the teacher's channel count by a trainable 1×1 conv (the projector). For a plain-ViT pretrained backbone the feature is the last tapped token map (after its tap norm); for other backbones it is the bottleneck. The teacher's feature is resized to the student's grid. alpha: 0 skips this term and builds no projector.
    • r(t) = 1 - t / T (decay: linear) fades both terms out over the run's optimizer steps, so the end of training is purely supervised. decay: none keeps them constant.

    loss and train/loss still log the supervised loss, so runs with and without distillation stay comparable. The distillation terms are logged as epoch means: train/distill_heatmap, train/distill_feat and train/distill_weight (r(t)). Validation is unchanged (val/loss is the supervised loss).

    Stage 2¶

    At the start of the run:

    1. The student is loaded from teacher (the stage-1 model; leave teacher unset to keep the weights model_config.init_from loaded).
    2. A frozen copy of the network becomes the teacher.
    3. Everything outside trainable (default [head]) is frozen, and trainable is re-initialized.

    The loss is gamma * L_heatmap, with no supervised loss (supervised_weight: 0, as in DWPose) and no decay (decay defaults to none at stage 2, since the distillation term is the whole loss). When the whole backbone is frozen it stays in eval mode (no drop path, no BatchNorm updates) and the teacher's head reads the student's own backbone features, so the backbone runs once per step.

    With a ViTPose-style head (vit_decoder: classic) the deconvolution decoder is part of the backbone, so trainable: [head] retrains only the final 1×1 conv. To retrain the decoder too:

    trainer_config:
  distillation:
    stage: 2
    teacher: /runs/vit_s_stage1
    trainable: [head, "backbone.vit_dec.*"]

    trainable takes the same selectors as model_config.init_from (see Selectors). Stage 2 cannot be combined with trainer_config.freeze_schedule.

    Box detectors (experimental)¶

    A trained box detector can teach another one, e.g. a large bbox_detr teaching a small bbox_detr or a bbox_ssd. Stage 1 only:

    trainer_config:
  distillation:
    teacher: /runs/detr_vit_b        # a trained bbox_* model directory
    teacher_score_threshold: 0.5     # teacher boxes kept as targets
    alpha: 1.0                       # feature loss weight (0 = off)
    beta: 1.0                        # weight of the loss against the teacher's boxes

    The loss is

    loss = supervised + r(t) * (alpha * L_feat + beta * L_box)
    • L_box is the student's own detection loss (its set criterion, SSD multibox loss or CenterNet loss) with the teacher's detections on the same training input as the targets: the teacher (frozen, eval mode) runs on the student's augmented frame, its boxes with a score of at least teacher_score_threshold are kept, and the student's target encoder turns them into targets. Box outputs do not line up between two detectors (set queries, anchors and center grids differ), so decoded boxes are what the two share: teacher and student may be different styles and input sizes. Categories are matched by name (a teacher with one category teaches a student with one category whatever the names); teacher boxes of other categories are dropped. The teacher must detect every student category: one it lacks would get only background targets from it, so setup refuses such a pair.
    • L_feat is as for pose models: the student's deepest backbone feature, projected to the teacher's channels, against the teacher's. For bbox_detr the feature is the backbone's last tapped token map.
    • teacher_input_scale resamples the student's frame for the teacher (rounded to a multiple of the teacher's max_stride); the teacher's boxes are scaled back. The teacher reads the student's preprocessed frame, so when its training preprocessing.scale, max_height or max_width differ from the student's (and teacher_input_scale is 1) setup warns: animals may not appear at the size the teacher trained on.
    • Logged as epoch means: train/distill_box, train/distill_feat, train/distill_teacher_boxes (teacher boxes per image used as targets) and train/distill_weight.

    This is the smallest working version: hard targets from the teacher's boxes, no soft class scores and no stage 2. The teacher's boxes are turned into targets on the CPU in each training step (not in the data loader workers); its cost on a GPU has not been measured. teacher_crop_size, gamma, trainable and mask_unlabeled_nodes do not apply. A teacher that misses animals the labels have, or finds animals they lack, passes that on, weighted by beta and fading with r(t); the supervised loss on the labels stays in full.

    Partially labeled data¶

    With per-node loss masking active (see Partially labeled skeletons), the heatmap term uses the same node_weights as the supervised loss: a node the sample's source did not label (or, under unlabeled_points: ignore, a node without a location) gets no teacher signal either. Set mask_unlabeled_nodes: false to distill every shared node everywhere, which is DWPose's choice: the teacher then fills in nodes the annotators left out.

    Teacher resolution¶

    A teacher trained at a higher resolution should see more pixels than the student. There are two ways to give it its input size.

    teacher_crop_size: a real high-resolution crop¶

    With preprocessing.crop_pipeline: warp (centered_instance), set teacher_crop_size to the teacher's input size, e.g. [384, 288] for a 384×288 teacher and a 256×192 student. The training loader then cuts every crop's window twice from the same decoded frame: once at crop_size for the student and once at teacher_crop_size for the teacher (teacher_image in the batch). The teacher sees the frame's own pixels at its resolution, not an upsampled student crop.

    Both crops come from one augmentation draw, so the two models always look at the same thing:

    • Geometry. Box jitter, half-body, rotation, scale, shift and flip build one affine; the teacher's is that affine followed by a rescale of the crop's pixel grid. The two crops cover exactly the same window, and the student's keypoint k is at (k + 0.5) * r - 0.5 in the teacher's crop (r = teacher_crop_size / crop_size).
    • Photometry. Contrast, brightness and HSV are the same lookup tables. Blur kernels and sigmas scale with r, and coarse-dropout holes and random erases cover the same part of the window. Noise is drawn at each crop's own resolution with the same strength.

    The student's crops and targets are identical with and without a teacher crop (the teacher's noise uses its own random stream). teacher_crop_size must have the aspect ratio of crop_size and be a multiple of the teacher's max_stride, and it cannot be combined with teacher_input_scale. Validation and inference are unchanged: only training batches carry teacher_image.

    The cost is a second warp and the photometric ops on the bigger crop: about 0.45 ms more CPU per sample in a loader worker (1.8 → 2.25 ms on COCO, 256×192 student, 384×288 teacher) and 3.3× the image bytes per batch. With 20 workers one loader still delivered ~7,500 samples/s (10,500 without the teacher crop), well above what a ViT-S rank consumes, so it should not slow training down; the teacher's own forward pass costs far more GPU time.

    Watch shared memory: batches in flight between the loader workers and the training process live in /dev/shm, about num_workers x prefetch_factor batches per GPU. With a 384×288 teacher crop a batch of 128 is ~61 MB instead of ~19 MB, so 20 workers with the default prefetch_factor: 2 hold ~2.5 GB per GPU (~10 GB on a 4-GPU node, twice that with prefetch_factor: 4). If workers die with a bus error or "No space left on device", lower trainer_config.train_data_loader.prefetch_factor or num_workers, or enlarge /dev/shm. The visualization datasets (visualize_preds_during_training) cut no teacher crops.

    teacher_input_scale: upsample the student's crop¶

    On the other crop paths, teacher_input_scale resamples the student's crop by that factor before it goes to the teacher (rounded to a multiple of the teacher's max_stride), and resizes the teacher's outputs back to the student's grids. This only upsamples the student's pixels: the teacher sees the same image content at a larger size, not extra detail.

    Either way, the teacher's outputs are resized back to the student's grids.

    The log warns when the teacher's heatmaps, after the resize, are much wider or narrower than the student's targets (sigma). A 384-crop teacher with sigma: 3 teaching a 256-crop student with sigma: 2 is consistent.

    The teacher at training time¶

    • It is frozen, in eval mode, and run under bf16 autocast on CUDA (teacher_precision: "32" runs it in the training precision instead).
    • It is not part of the student module: it is not in the state_dict, the optimizer, EMA or checkpoints. It follows the student across devices.
    • Only the teacher's model_config is read, so teachers trained with data or trainer options this version lacks still load. Its checkpoint state_dict is loaded as is: for a teacher trained with weight EMA, that is the EMA weights.
    • Under DDP each rank loads its own copy; DDP neither wraps nor syncs it. The projector is a normal trainable parameter and is all-reduced like the rest of the student.

    The projector is training-only. Checkpoints keep it under checkpoint["distillation"] rather than in state_dict, so they load into a plain model for inference, export or init_from with no extra keys. Resuming a distillation run (resume_ckpt_path) restores it. Resuming stage 2 needs teacher set, since the resumed weights are not the teacher.

    Options¶

    Key Default Description
    teacher null Teacher model directory or .ckpt next to its training_config.yaml. Required for stage 1
    stage 1 1 teacher → student, 2 head self-distillation
    alpha 1.0 Feature loss weight (stage 1); 0 disables it
    beta 1.0 Heatmap loss weight (stage 1)
    gamma 1.0 Heatmap loss weight (stage 2)
    supervised_weight null Supervised loss weight; null = 1 at stage 1, 0 at stage 2
    decay null linear (r(t) = 1 - t/T) or none; null = linear at stage 1, none at stage 2
    teacher_input_scale 1.0 Student crop → teacher input resampling factor
    mask_unlabeled_nodes true Weight the heatmap term by node_weights
    trainable null Stage 2: what is re-initialized and trained ([head])
    teacher_precision bf16 bf16 or 32
    teacher_score_threshold 0.5 Box detectors: teacher detections with at least this score are the distillation targets

    The feature and heatmap losses have different scales: heatmap MSE is about the size of the supervised loss (~1e-3), while the feature MSE of LayerNorm'd ViT tokens starts near 0.05–1. alpha and beta are not tuned yet; check the train/distill_* logs to see what each term contributes.
